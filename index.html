The same every time

Same name and same pulse always give the same winners, for you and for everyone else. Change one character of the name and the winners are completely different.

Registered in advance

Reproducing the numbers is not enough on its own. Anyone holding a published pulse can try names until one crowns the entrant they wanted, and every one of those results verifies perfectly.
